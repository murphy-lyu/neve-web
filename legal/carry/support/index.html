Carry Support

Trip planning and packing help

Contact us

Email support@nevestudio.app. We usually reply within 7 business days.

Before you write

Please include your Carry version, iOS version, device model, and the steps that reproduce the issue. Do not send health information, identity documents, booking documents, or other sensitive attachments.

iCloud sync

Carry does not require an in-app account. Sync uses the private iCloud account signed in on your device. Check that both devices use the same Apple Account, iCloud Drive is enabled, and Carry is allowed to use iCloud. Changes may take a short time to appear.

Weather and Health features

Destination forecasts require a network connection. Period-aware packing reminders are optional, off by default, and process read-only Cycle Tracking data on your device.
